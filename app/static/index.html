    How it works

    1. Clean-up. Disguised spellings are undone (l@nja → lanja, gooood → good), links and @handles are removed.
    2. Word list. A message with a severe abusive word and no earlier messages is flagged straight away. Milder or ambiguous words are only noted.
    3. Model. Everything else is read by a fine-tuned IndicBERT model together with up to three earlier messages. It scores five kinds of harm: toxic, severe, identity attack, insult and threat.
    4. Decision. A message is flagged when its score passes a line tuned on validation data. Scores close to that line are marked Needs review.

    Limits

    • The Telugu training data is small; Tamil code-mixed comments and translated prompts fill the gap. Expect mistakes on slang the model has not seen.
    • The highlighted words show where the model's attention went, which is a hint, not a proof of why it decided.
    • Use it to support a human moderator, not to replace one.
